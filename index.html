About us

Fresh produce, bought well and sold fairly

Thara Ground Trading Company is a wholesale fruit and vegetable supplier in the Dammam vegetable market. We buy fresh every day and sell to the trade at wholesale prices.

When the market needs it, we also bring in selected imported produce.

To be the first name buyers across the Eastern Province think of for fresh produce.

Our mission
